# Maintenance status

UST is a research software release by Chirag Srinivas. Further development,
support, response times and compatibility updates are not promised.

You may report issues and propose changes. A response or merge is not
guaranteed. This is not a supported commercial product.

The MSc archive is a fixed historical snapshot. Corrections must be published
as separately versioned material with an explanation; do not silently replace
the original evidence. The reusable toolkit may receive updates independently,
but no update programme is currently committed.
